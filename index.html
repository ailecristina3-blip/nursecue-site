About NurseCue

NurseCue is a nursing workflow and documentation support tool designed to help nurses capture important clinical events during busy shifts.

The app allows users to record brief voice notes, convert recordings into text, organize clinical event information, create structured nursing note drafts, and keep track of events requiring follow-up or documentation.

NurseCue is a workflow and documentation support tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations and is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment, institutional policies, or required medical record documentation.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

NurseCue is designed to assist users with capturing and organizing information for nursing workflow and documentation purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using NurseCue.

Voice Recordings and Transcription

NurseCue allows users to create audio recordings for transcription. When a user chooses to transcribe a recording, the audio is transmitted to NurseCue's server infrastructure and third-party service providers used to process the recording and return transcribed text.

NurseCue currently uses Render for server infrastructure and OpenAI services for transcription and generation of nursing note drafts. Information submitted for these features may therefore be processed by these service providers as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Audio uploaded to the NurseCue server for transcription is deleted from the NurseCue server after the transcription request is processed.

Users should not intentionally enter patient-identifying information unless their use of NurseCue and the services involved has been authorized by their organization and complies with applicable privacy, security, and healthcare requirements. NurseCue should not be assumed to be HIPAA compliant solely because information is deleted after processing.

Generated Documentation

NurseCue may process transcribed information using third-party services to create a structured nursing note draft. Generated content is intended to assist with documentation and must be reviewed and verified by the user for accuracy before being entered into an official health record.

Information Stored on the Device

NurseCue may store clinical event information, recordings, transcripts, generated note drafts, reminder information, and related app data on the user's device so that the user can return to saved events.

After a user confirms that a note has been entered and signed in the official electronic health record (EHR), NurseCue provides a "Documented & Delete" function that removes the saved event from NurseCue, including its locally saved recording, transcript, and generated note draft.

Microphone Access

NurseCue requests microphone access for its audio recording features. Recording begins only after the user initiates the recording function. Microphone access can be controlled through the device's privacy settings.

Notifications

NurseCue may request permission to send notifications for clinical workflow reminders created through the app. Notification permissions can be managed through the device's settings.

Third-Party Services

NurseCue relies on third-party service providers to support certain app functions, including server hosting, audio transcription, and generation of nursing note drafts. These providers may process information transmitted through NurseCue as necessary to perform those services.

Medical Advice

NurseCue is a documentation-assistance tool and does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or emergency medical services. Clinical decisions and official documentation remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NurseCue develops or its features, service providers, and data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
